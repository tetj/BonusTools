
There are 2 main sections :
A) Games I've tried (with my kids)
B) Games I've not tried yet

These 2 sections are divided into 3 main sub sections :
i) 2 players max
ii) up to 4 players and more
iii) online multiplayer

I've started to add the minimal age I recommend. If no age is speciied, then the minimal age is 6-7yo. All games are available on Nintendo Switch unless specified. I try to focus on games that gets above average reviews. I'm probably going to put this in an Excel spreadsheet at some point to make it easier to filter. Games with less than 300 reviews on Steam and less than 20 reviews on Metacritic gets the "LK" tag which stands for lesser-known.
